More than a planner A creative partner

With fourteen years in luxury weddings across Europe, I specialise in celebrations that feel deeply personal and effortlessly elegant never a template, always you.

I founded Elara Events in 2013, built on a simple belief your wedding should feel like you, not a template. I work with an intimate circle of trusted vendors across Europe, each sharing my obsession with quality.

Fourteen years later, 280+ celebrations, six countries and still, every new couple brings something I haven't seen before. That's the part that keeps me going.
